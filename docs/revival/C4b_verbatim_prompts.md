Distilled strictly from his words above — concrete wishes/decisions, each with his quote as
evidence. Nothing invented.

1. **etceter4 IS a visual portfolio — the "visual representation" of his photo/video/multimedia
   work — not a tip-jar storefront.** His own statement of the deliverable:
   > "what i really want is a visual representation of my photographic video multimedia work"
   and the explicit anti-pattern:
   > "i don't want $1 tips--that's for the guy on the side of the highway".
   → The temple is a *gallery/showcase*, not a checkout page.

2. **It is the public face of an artist-academic — art history, studies, "constructions."**
   He defines his own center of gravity:
   > "at my heart im an academic and an artist … youll see my interest in art history and doing
   > studies and visual arts and the arts and constructions in general".
   → Visual language should read as curatorial / scholarly (studies of "techniques and
   processes"), not as a generic dev-portfolio. This is the seed of the
   Akademia/Bibliotheke/Pinakotheke/Odeion "chambers."

   deal-flow, not donations.** His causal model:
   > "a github repo … brought us a potential client 3-4 months ago and it was bc i built a product

   and he ranks "**B2B deal-flow from the live portfolio**" as the "NEAR-TERM CASH · matches
   ambition · my lane" option while saying "my instinct is **all of it**". → The site must be
   *undeniable presence* / proof-of-power, built to a "greatest on Earth" standard.

4. **Replace/own the social funnel.** He is already pulling traffic on Instagram and wants to
   convert it to something he controls:
   > "im currently putting stuff on my instagram, and im getting 3.600 profile views this month,
   > i should be linking to github or what i really want is a visual representation…"
