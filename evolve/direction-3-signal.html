On the grain of the image: analogue noise as compositional material

Signal decay: glitch aesthetics from Nam June Paik to datamoshing

About

Anthony
Padavano

Anthony Padavano is an artist and academic whose practice moves between photography, film and video, generative and glitch art, sound composition, and critical writing. His work is concerned with the edges of systems — where signal becomes noise, where the documentary eye slips into abstraction, where technology reveals the grain of its own construction.

Working across analog and digital registers, his image-making draws on a lineage that includes structural film, ambient music, and the literary modernism of Joyce and Woolf. The result is a practice organized less by medium than by sensibility: dense, precise, warm, and in sustained conversation with its antecedents.
