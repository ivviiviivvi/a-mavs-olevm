04

About

Anthony
Padavano

Anthony Padavano is an academic and artist whose practice moves between photography, film and video, multimedia installation, generative and glitch art, and sound composition. His work attends to the textures of the overlooked — performance light, the materiality of the scan, the rhythm inside a static frame. Trained in art history and media studies, he treats practice and scholarship as a single inquiry, building a body of work that is intellectually serious and formally precise.
