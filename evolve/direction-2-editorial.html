About

Anthony Padavano is an artist and academic working across photography, film, multimedia installation, generative and glitch art, sound composition, and writing. His practice is rooted in the tension between structure and accident — in the way a deliberate system (a camera, an algorithm, a score) encounters the world and is transformed by it.

He holds academic appointments in the humanities, where his scholarly work interrogates image theory, media history, and the phenomenology of recorded experience. His studio practice runs alongside and through that scholarship: the studies published here are neither purely academic nor purely artistic — they are both.

He is based in the United States. His photographic and multimedia work is distributed across the series documented in this portfolio: Diary, Faster, Media, Live, Slip, and Glitchpr0n. All four pillars — Vision, Sound, Words, and the ongoing inquiry of Studies — constitute a single, continuous project.
